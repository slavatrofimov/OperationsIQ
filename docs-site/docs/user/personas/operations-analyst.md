# Operations analyst

You dig deeper into the data to understand behavior, discover patterns, and
inform decisions.

## A typical workflow

1. **Explore broadly.** Start in [Explore → Overview](/user/explore/overview),
   then use [Trend & volatility](/user/explore/trend-volatility),
   [Spectrum](/user/explore/spectrum), and [Heatmaps](/user/explore/heatmaps) to
   characterize a signal.
2. **Find recurrences.** Brush a shape and run
   [Similarity search](/user/explore/similarity-search), or let
   [Matrix Profile](/user/patterns/matrix-profile) discover motifs and anomalies
   over long histories.
3. **Explain relationships.** Use the
   [Influence map](/user/diagnose/influence-map) and
   [Regression & sensitivity](/user/diagnose/regression-sensitivity) to quantify
   how signals relate.
4. **Model & classify.** Label examples and train
   [Classifiers](/user/patterns/classifiers) for repeatable recognition.
5. **Project forward.** Use [Forecast](/user/planning/forecast) and
   [What-if](/user/planning/what-if) for planning.
6. **Report.** Assemble findings into an [Investigation](/user/investigations) —
   charts, data, and searchable notes included.

## Tips

- Use [Decomposition](/user/diagnose/decomposition) to separate trend, seasonality,
  and residual before anomaly work.
- Derive new indicators in [Derived metrics](/user/explore/derived-metrics) when
  the measurement you need isn't stored directly.
