# Trend & volatility

This module surfaces the **direction** (trend) and **variability** (volatility)
of a signal over time, rather than just its raw values.

## When to use it

- You want to know whether a measurement is drifting up or down.
- You care about how *noisy* or *stable* a signal is, and when that changes.
- You're looking for periods of unusual instability.

## How to use it

1. Select a signal and time range.
2. Review the trend and volatility views together — a stable mean can still hide
   rising volatility, and vice versa.

## Reading the results

- **Trend** highlights sustained movement in one direction.
- **Volatility** highlights how much the signal is fluctuating; rising volatility
  often precedes instability or faults.
